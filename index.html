Simple from first call to final files.

04 · FAQ

Common questions.

Where does Fieldline Systems work?+

We fly throughout Pennsylvania and New Jersey. We're based in Bucks County, PA, and regularly work across the greater Philadelphia region. Larger projects outside that area are welcome. Just ask.

How quickly will I get my photos, video or data?+

Edited photos and video are usually delivered within 48 hours of the flight. Field scans and analysis usually take 2 to 3 days. Larger or multi visit projects get a delivery schedule up front.

Are you FAA licensed?+

Yes. Every flight is flown by an FAA Part 107 certified remote pilot, flying Remote ID compliant aircraft, with FAA airspace authorization through LAANC or FAA DroneZone obtained whenever a site is in controlled airspace. A copy of the flight plan and any FAA authorization comes with your deliverables.

Do you fly over people or neighboring properties?+

We plan every flight to follow FAA rules, which means we don't fly over people who aren't directly participating in the flight, including work crews, homeowners and passersby. On active job sites we coordinate timing and flight paths with the site lead. We keep flights focused on your property, and we check airspace, temporary flight restrictions and local rules before we arrive.

Can a drone inspect my roof without anyone climbing a ladder?+

Yes. A drone roof inspection captures close, high resolution photos of shingles, flashing, gutters and solar panels without ladders or lifts, and gives you a clear record for contractors or insurance adjusters.

Can you document a construction project from start to finish?+

Yes. We schedule recurring construction progress flights from groundbreaking to final walkthrough, using consistent angles so owners, lenders and project teams can compare progress over time.

What happens if the weather doesn't cooperate?+

We only fly in safe conditions within FAA limits. If wind, rain or low visibility rules out a flight, we reschedule at no extra cost.
