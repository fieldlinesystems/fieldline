01 · Services

From a single flyover to a measured 3D model.

Every job is planned around what you need to show, prove or build from, and delivered in formats ready for your site, social or project file.

Aerial photo & video

Listing aerials for residential and commercial real estate, plus marketing stills, twilight shots and cinematic flyovers cut for your website, social and ads.

Construction progress

Scheduled flyovers from groundbreaking to final walkthrough, giving owners, lenders and project teams one shared record of where the job stands.

Inspections & storm damage

Roof, solar and structure inspections without ladders or lifts, and fast post storm documentation for owners, adjusters and contractors.

3D scanning & reality capture

LiDAR and photogrammetry that turn buildings and sites into accurate point clouds and 3D models, with scan to CAD and BIM deliverables in Revit or MicroStation.

Mapping & precise measurement

Orthomosaic site maps, elevations, stockpile volumes and as built measurements, processed with survey grade tools and checked against control.

Ag & landscape

Field scans that show which zones are thriving and which need attention, so treatment goes where it matters instead of blanket coverage. For farms, vineyards, golf courses and large grounds.
